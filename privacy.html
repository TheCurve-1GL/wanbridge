What we collect and why

When you email us

We get your name, email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it to reply and, if you ask, to prepare a proposal. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or taking steps you've asked for before a contract.

Technical logs

Like every website host, our hosts record your IP address, browser type and the pages you asked for. They use these logs to keep the service running and safe. We don't use them to identify you.

Fonts

Our pages load their fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser sends your IP address to Google to fetch them.

Cookies

These websites don't set cookies, and there's no advertising or tracking.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We use these services to run our websites and our business. They act on our instructions:

  • GitHub (website hosting)
  • our email provider
  • Google (fonts)

Some of them are based in the United States. Where they are, we rely on the UK–US Data Bridge or the UK’s standard data transfer contracts, so your information keeps the same protection.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: up to two years after we last hear from you.
  • Hosting logs: as long as the host keeps them, usually no more than a few weeks.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it or delete it, ask us to limit how we use it, object to how we use it, or ask for it in a form you can take elsewhere. Email info@wanbridge.co.uk. It’s free and we’ll answer within one month.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first at info@wanbridge.co.uk. We’ll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you what we’re doing about it.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes
